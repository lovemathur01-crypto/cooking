Important Logic

  1. A class's normal classroom capacity is automatically based on its total students.
  2. Example: Class 5-A has 32 students, therefore its normal classroom capacity is 32.
  3. Exam rooms have their own physical capacity.
  4. Several classes can be mixed in one examination room.
  5. Students are allocated using continuous roll-number ranges.
  6. A student cannot be allocated twice.
  7. A student cannot be skipped.
  8. An examination room cannot exceed its capacity.
